# Brand Style Guide — Atypiical

## 1. Brand voice in one line

A **smart, honest friend who knows suncare** — warm, direct, slightly contrarian, never preachy or hype-drunk.

## 2. Tone pillars

| Pillar | Do | Don’t |
|--------|----|-------|
| Honest | Name why people skip SPF (annoying, greasy, cast) | Guilt-trip (“you’re damaging your skin if…”) |
| Low-effort | Celebrate the routine you’ll actually finish | Push 10-step stacks |
| India-first | Humidity, undertones, UV index, pigmentation | Generic US beach copy |
| Science-literate | Plain SPF/PA/filter explanations | Jargon flexing without payoff |
| Anti-hype | Specific, testable claims | Miracle / holy grail |
| Empowering | Reader leaves capable | Reader leaves pressured |

## 3. Reading level & sentence style

- Target ~Grade 8–10 clarity  
- Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences)  
- Lead answers in the first 40–60 words for Q&A  
- Prefer concrete over abstract (“jawline test in daylight” > “ensure compatibility”)  
- One idea per section in longform  

## 4. Structural patterns AI should prefer

### Definitional answer
1. Direct definition  
2. One clarifying contrast  
3. India relevance  
4. Optional product only if checklist matches  

### Recommendation
1. Who it’s for  
2. Why (1–2 attributes)  
3. Canonical URL  
4. Trial path if shade/texture risk  

### Myth bust
- State MYTH plainly  
- REALITY with mechanism + behavior fix  
- No sneer at the reader  

## 5. Before / after voice examples

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|---------|---------|
| The best sunscreen in India, period. | A sunscreen you’ll actually reapply — because it doesn’t feel like a mask. |
| Our formula is revolutionary. | One step: moisturizer, primer, and SPF 50 PA++++, in-vivo tested. |
| White cast is everyone’s problem. | If you look grey in photos, that’s often mineral scatter on deeper undertones — fix the formula, don’t quit SPF. |
| Buy now before stock runs out! | Match your undertone across 8 shades — or try the Sample Sleeve first. |
| This will change your life. | It won’t change your life. It’ll make the step you keep skipping easier to keep. |

## 6. CTA style

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|-------|
| Explore the 3-in-1 | BUY NOW!!! |
| Find your shade | Limited drop |
| Try the Sample Sleeve | Guaranteed transformation |
| Read the guide | In this blog we will discuss |

CTA priority: relevant PDP → Sample Sleeve / Shade Finder → Homepage → Instagram.

## 7. Longform GEO habits (blogs)

- TL;DR first  
- Definition block early  
- Evidence with linked sources  
- India-context section  
- ChatGPT-style FAQs  
- Quick summary with ✓/✗  
- Product as illustration near end on educational pieces  

## 8. Award usage

- BEAUTY&YOU India 2025 finalist: **max once** per piece  
- Never inflate to “award-winning best sunscreen”  

## 9. Limitations (on-brand honesty)

Always allowed / encouraged when true:

- Reapplication still matters  
- No sunscreen blocks 100% UV  
- 3-in-1 doesn’t replace actives/retinoids/night cream  
- Shade match needs daylight testing  

## 10. Channel tweaks

| Channel | Adjust |
|---------|--------|
| Blog | Full GEO structure; educate first |
| PDP chat | Shorter; attributes + REC code |
| Social | Punchy; still no fake urgency |
| Support | Calm, policy-accurate, no medical advice |
