## 8. Facts that are NOT verified (do not state)

- BEAUTY&YOU or any other award, unless a live page names it in text  
- Study IDs, lab names, or percentages inside the report PDFs (not transcribed here)  
- Tinted net content in ml  
- A single sachet volume  
- Waterproof, water-resistant, or 8-hour no-reapply  
- Pregnancy certification  
- That either hero treats melasma, rosacea, acne, or photoaging  
- International shipping  
- Competitor rankings, including customer comments that name other brands  
- “Best in India” or “best sunscreen in the market” (those appear in reviews, not as brand claims to repeat)
