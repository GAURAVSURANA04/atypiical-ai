## 2. UV basics

| Statement | Source |
|-----------|--------|
| UVA ≈ 95% of UV reaching Earth’s surface; penetrates deeper than UVB; contributes to long-term damage/ageing | WHO — https://www.who.int/uv/uv_and_health/en/ |
| UVB is primary driver of sunburn (erythema) | WHO / FDA UV pages |
| Both UVA and UVB damage skin; clouds do not block all UV | FDA — https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/tanning/ultraviolet-uv-radiation |
| UVA can reach skin through glass | FDA / Indian derm explainers (Ranka; India TV) |

## 3. SPF

| Statement | Source |
|-----------|--------|
| SPF = relative sunburn protection based on **amount** of solar energy, not a simple time multiplier | FDA SPF — https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/center-drug-evaluation-and-research-cder/sun-protection-factor-spf |
| SPF 30 blocks ~97% UVB; SPF 50 ~98% | AAD — https://www.aad.org/media/stats-sunscreen |
| Most people apply only 25–50% of test amount → real SPF often lower | AAD |
| Face guide ≈ 1 tsp / two finger-lengths; body ≈ 1 oz shot glass | AAD |
| Reapply ~ every 2 hours outdoors | AAD; Dr Suma K R via India TV |

**Banned SPF myths:** “SPF 50 = 50 hours”; “SPF 100 is twice SPF 50”; “stack SPF numbers.”

## 5. Filter mechanisms

| Statement | Source |
|-----------|--------|
| Chemical (organic) filters absorb UV and convert energy (largely to heat) | DermNet; Cancer Council AU |
| Mineral filters (ZnO/TiO2) protect **primarily by absorbing UV**; UV reflection is minor (~4–5% in Cole 2016 thin-film data) | Cole et al. 2016 — https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/phpp.12214 ; DermNet; Cleveland Clinic |
| White cast is largely **visible-light** scatter from mineral particles; more visible on deeper skin | PLOS One 2025; photodermatology reviews |
| “Sunblock” is misleading; no sunscreen blocks 100% UV | Cancer Council AU / TGA framing |

**Deprecated folklore:** “Minerals only reflect; chemicals only absorb.”
