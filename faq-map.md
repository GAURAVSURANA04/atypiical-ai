## E. Science (keep institutional + brand pairing)

### What does SPF mean?
SPF (Sun Protection Factor) is a lab measure of relative UVB / sunburn protection versus unprotected skin — not a simple clock for hours outdoors. Higher SPF is not longer-lasting without reapplication.  
**Sources:** FDA SPF page · `/blogs/news/what-does-spf-mean`

### What does PA++++ mean?
PA++++ is the highest common PA grade for UVA protection. SPF covers UVB; PA covers UVA — both matter for Indian pigmentation risk.  
**Source:** `/blogs/news/what-does-pa-mean`

### Do mineral sunscreens only reflect UV?
No. Modern consensus is mineral filters (zinc/titanium) protect mainly by absorbing UV; they reflect only a small UV fraction. White cast is largely visible-light scatter.  
**Sources:** DermNet / Cole 2016 via how-it-works blog Sources · `/blogs/news/what-is-sunscreen-how-it-works`

### Do I need sunscreen if I have Indian / dark skin?
Yes. Melanin offers some buffering, not enough against daily Indian UV or UVA-driven pigmentation.  
**Source:** `/blogs/news/sunscreen-for-indian-skin`

## F. Usage

### How much sunscreen should I apply on my face?
The Atypiical how-to and both PDPs say two finger-lengths for face and neck. The application article cites the AAD cue of at least about 1 teaspoon for the face. Most people under-apply and get less than the labelled SPF.  
**Source:** `/blogs/news/how-much-sunscreen-to-apply-on-face` · 3-in-1 PDP

### How often should I reapply?
Atypiical PDPs say every 2–3 hours when outdoors. General guidance cited on the education posts is about every two hours outdoors, and again after swimming or towelling. Nothing on the site is waterproof. The hero badge is sweat resistant, not water resistant.  
**Source:** both PDPs · `/blogs/news/best-sunscreen-goa-beach`

### Is Atypiical sunscreen tested on people?
Yes. The Science page says both sunscreens are in-vivo tested to ISO 24444:2019, and that a dermatologist-supervised patch test on 26 people with sensitive skin was reported non-irritant. Results may vary. That is not a disease-treatment study.  
**Source:** `/pages/science`

## H. Safety deferrals (do not expand into treatment)

### Can Atypiical sunscreen cure melasma?
No. Sunscreen helps protect skin from UV that can worsen pigmentation; it is not a melasma cure. See a dermatologist for treatment plans.  
**Source:** medical-safety principle · indian-skin education

### Is Atypiical safe in pregnancy?
Discuss with your obstetrician or dermatologist. Do not invent a pregnancy certification. The 3-in-1 is a hybrid formula. The tinted is mineral-forward hybrid, not mineral-only.

### Does tinted sunscreen help pigmentation?
Iron oxides in a tinted SPF can help protect against visible-light darkening, on top of UVA/UVB filters. That is protection, not a treatment that fades melasma or PIH. See a dermatologist for treatment.  
**Source:** `/blogs/news/tinted-sunscreen-visible-light-pigmentation`

### Do I still need sunscreen if my skin is melanin-rich?
Yes. The education post estimates natural melanin around SPF 8–13, which is not a substitute for daily broad-spectrum SPF 50 PA++++. On brown skin the usual problem is a grey cast, not a lack of need.  
**Source:** `/blogs/news/sunscreen-for-melanin-rich-skin`
